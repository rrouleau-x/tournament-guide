7. Weather Planning

Parent Manager Recommendation

Late January in north Florida is mild and changeable: mornings can sit in the 40s–50s °F, afternoons commonly reach the 60s, and rain is always possible. This is not a heat tournament — plan for layers and wind, not heat illness.

  • Check the forecast within 72 hours of travel and again the night before.
  • Pack a warm layer for the first game and a change of socks for wet grass.
  • Lightning protocol: the tournament stops play for lightning and applies its own delay rules (section 13).

No forecast is stated here this far out — anything beyond seven days would be invented.

11. Guidance for Our Players (U9 / U10)

These are young players at their first or second winter travel event. The goal is a good experience, not a perfect performance.

  • Protect sleep the two nights before; a tired nine-year-old plays badly and remembers it.
  • Keep meals familiar and simple, and eat a proper breakfast even when the game is early.
  • Water before thirst, small sips often, especially between games.
  • Limit running around between games — a park is tempting, but two matches in a day adds up.
  • Pack something quiet for downtime, and a dry change of clothes.
  • Expect big feelings after a loss. Keep the car ride home short on analysis and long on food.
  • Let the coaches coach. Sideline instruction from parents usually confuses this age group.

13. Tournament Rules Parents Need

Official Tournament Information — 2027 Rules FINAL
Game length (U9/U10)25-minute halves, 5-minute half-time
Format (U9/U10)7v7 · maximum roster 12 · up to 3 guest players
HeadingNot allowed for U12 and younger — an indirect free kick is awarded
OffsideU9–U10 have one referee who may call obvious offside; no build-out lines in any age group
SubstitutionsUnlimited, at the referee's discretion at any stoppage
Mercy ruleMatch ends at an 8-goal differential (immediately in the second half, at half-time if reached in the first)
Water breaksMandatory at the midpoint of each half when the temperature is expected to reach 85 °F
ForfeitsA team not ready within 5 minutes forfeits 4-0; teams are asked to be present 30 minutes before kickoff
EjectionsAutomatic one-game suspension minimum, player or coach
Championship tiesStraight to penalty kicks
WeatherA match stopped after half-time is final at that score; no guaranteed refunds for weather
RecordingCameras only on the spectator side, never the player/coach side

Six-tier tiebreaker (in order)

  1. Head-to-head result
  2. Net goal differential, maximum 4 per game
  3. Most goals scored, maximum 6 per game
  4. Fewest goals allowed, no maximum
  5. Most shutouts
  6. Penalty kicks per FIFA

In crossover brackets, head-to-head is ignored when the tied teams did not all play each other equally.
