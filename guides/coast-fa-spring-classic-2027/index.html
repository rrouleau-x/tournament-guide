11. Guidance for Our Players (U9 / U10)

  • Protect sleep the two nights before.
  • Familiar meals, a proper breakfast.
  • Water before thirst.
  • Limit running around between games.
  • Quiet activities and a dry change of clothes for downtime.
  • Keep the ride home short on analysis.
  • Let the coaches coach.
